01 · Welcome to Blue Hole Villas

Barefoot days on the quiet side of Andros

Tucked among coconut palms on the Queen’s Highway in Congo Town, Blue Hole Villas is a small collection of individually shaped island villas set on a secluded private beach — five minutes from the airport, and a world away from everything else.

Across the street, ancient blue holes sink into the pine forest. Just offshore, a living reef keeps the shallows calm and clear. Your days here are measured in tides, not appointments — and your hosts are happy to arrange everything from fly-fishing guides to a wedding barefoot in the sand.

  • Each villa sleeps two adults — or a small family of four
  • Full kitchens made for slow mornings and long stays
  • WiFi throughout, when you feel like checking in
  • Weddings & special occasions arranged with your hosts

04 · Blue Holes & Nature

An ancient wonder, steps away

Blue holes are flooded sinkholes and underwater cave systems — windows into the island itself, shifting from pale jade at the rim to impossible sapphire at the center. On South Andros they are simply part of the neighbourhood: one lies directly across the Queen’s Highway from the villas, with more scattered through the forest and creeks beyond.

Directly across the street

Blue holes sit just steps from your villa — no tour bus, no ticket line.

Swim & explore

Andros holds one of the highest concentrations of blue holes on the planet, from hidden inland pools to vast ocean caverns.

Wild Andros

Pine forest, coppice and empty roads — the largest, least-developed island in The Bahamas.

05 · Fishing · Kayaking · Snorkeling

Days measured in tides

Water sports of every kind belong to this coast. Tell us what you love, and your hosts will arrange the rest.

Guides arranged

Fly-Fishing

South Andros is bonefish country — endless flats, tailing fish, and tides that set the day’s rhythm. We arrange fly-fishing trips with experienced local guides.

By arrangement

Kayaking

Slip into glassy creeks and mangrove channels where the water runs clear over white sand. Kayaking is available by arrangement, right from the property.

Just offshore

Snorkeling & the Reef

A living reef shelters our waters just offshore, keeping the shallows calm and full of life. Mask, fins, and you’re floating over coral minutes from your door.

Bonefishing at dawn, a reef before lunch, kayaking at golden hour — one conversation with your hosts and it’s on the calendar.
