01About Sonsette Villas

Barefoot calm,
quietly kept

Sonsette Villas is a small collection of Caribbean-style villas set among the palms of Major's Cay, on Crooked Island — a quiet Out Island of The Bahamas where the loudest sound most evenings is the tide.

Each villa rests roughly 55 feet from the Atlantic Ocean, close enough to fall asleep to the swell and wake to horizon views with your morning coffee. Nothing here is rushed, and nothing is crowded.

Your host, Ms. Alsette Deleveaux, keeps everything personal and simple — one call or one email, and your island plans begin.

04Island Life

Crooked Island keeps
its own time

This is the real Out Islands — no crowds, no schedules, no noise. Days stretch out between the palms and the shallows, and the island politely insists you slow down to meet it.

The nearest restaurant sits about eight miles up the road, so evenings tend to be quiet ones: something simple from the kitchenette, the sound of the ocean, and a sunset that refuses to hurry. That's not a compromise here — it's the whole point.

Slow mornings

Sunrise over the Atlantic, coffee on the porch, nowhere to be.

Empty beaches

Long walks where the only footprints in the sand are your own.

Trade-wind evenings

Dinner in from the kitchenette, then a sky full of stars.
