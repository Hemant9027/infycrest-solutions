01About Morris Motel

A little motel with a straight answer.

Morris Motel has one job: give travellers a clean, comfortable and genuinely affordable place to sleep in Nassau. We're a small, independent, locally run motel on Davis Street — not a resort, and proud of it.

Because we're small, everything is personal. You book by talking to us directly, you get honest answers about what's available and what it costs, and you get the kind of local tips that never make it into the brochures.

Locally run

An independent motel — decisions happen here, not at a head office.

Affordable by design

02Rooms & your stay

Comfort first.
Prices that behave.

Rooms at Morris Motel are simple, well-kept and easy on the budget — made for travellers who come to Nassau for the island, not for the thread count. Every stay starts the same way: you tell us what you need, we tell you honestly what we have.

How rooms & rates work

We don't publish fixed room types or prices online — availability and rates change with the season. Send your dates and group size and we'll confirm exactly what's open, what it costs and what's included. Straight answers, no fine print.

04Explore Nassau

Out the door, into the island.

Morris Motel sits on Davis Street in central Nassau — a handy jumping-off point for the island's everyday favourites. When you arrive, ask us how to get around. Pointing guests the right way is half the job.

Bay Street & the cruise port

Downtown Nassau's shops, straw crafts and harbour views — the island at its brightest and busiest.
