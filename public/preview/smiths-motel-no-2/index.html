About No. 2

The second Smith’s — with a spirit all its own.

Smith’s Motel No. 2 is an independent motel in Nassau, Bahamas — the little sibling to Smith’s Motel No. 1. Same family name, same friendly welcome, but its own corner of the island and its own laid-back rhythm.

We keep island stays the way they should be: simple, modern and easy on the budget, with Caribbean colour in every corner and a genuine hello at the door.

Nassau Guide

Our island favourites.

Nassau is a bright tangle of history, colour and very easy beach days. Here’s where we’d start — and when you arrive, just ask. Local tips are our favourite amenity.

New Providence, Bahamas
Sun & swim

The beaches

Powder-soft sand and water that barely looks real — the reason 'just one more day' exists.

Eat like a local

Arawak Cay · the Fish Fry

Conch salad made fresh in front of you, music drifting between the stalls. Come hungry, leave happy.

Wander & browse

Downtown & Bay Street

Pastel storefronts, cheerful chaos and the famous Straw Market — bring your bargaining smile.

Island rhythm

Junkanoo

The Bahamas' heartbeat — drums, cowbells and dazzling costumes flooding the streets on festival nights.

All beloved Nassau classics. Getting around New Providence is easy — taxis and the local “jitney” buses criss-cross the island all day.
