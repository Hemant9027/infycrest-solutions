01 — The Lodge

A slower way to spend your days

Nathan’s Lodge sits on the quiet side of South Andros — an island of pine forest, hidden blue holes and water in more shades of blue than you can name. It is a place kept simple on purpose: an authentic island escape for travelers who would rather wade a flat at sunrise than queue for anything.

Days here run on tide and sunlight. Coffee as the sky turns brass, sand between your toes by noon, long suppers and longer conversations after dark. No schedule is handed to you — the island suggests one, gently.

02 — Stay

Rooms made for ocean air

The lodge keeps its rooms the way the island keeps its beaches — unhurried and unbuttoned. Expect rustic comfort with a light touch: somewhere cool to rest between the flats and the table, with character in the wood grain.

Every stay is arranged directly with us, so tell us how you like to travel and we’ll shape the rest around it.

03 — Island Life

The island sets the pace

South Andros is the Out Islands at their most honest — scattered settlements, hand-painted signs, and people who wave because that’s simply what you do. Conch is cracked a few steps from the sea, baskets are woven by hand, and evenings end when the light gives out.

Borrow the rhythm: cycle the sandy lanes, stop where the smoke smells good, and let a stranger become the best part of your afternoon. This is island life you’re invited into — not performed for you.

04 — Fishing & Adventure

Flats, reefs & blue water

South Andros rests at the southern end of the island anglers call the Bonefishing Capital of the World — but between the tides there are reefs, creeks and blue holes waiting too. Guided days are arranged through the lodge; tell us what you’re dreaming of.

The main reason people find us

Flats & Bonefish

Wade or pole the shallows with local guides who read tide, light and wind like a living map. First cast or five-thousandth — the flats reward patience.

Half day or long day

Reef & Deep Water

Snapper and grouper over the reef edge, bigger stories out along the drop into the Tongue of the Ocean.

Slow exploration

Creeks & Blue Holes

Paddle quiet mangrove creeks, plunge into freshwater blue holes, snorkel water so clear boats seem to float on air.

“The water out here has a dozen colors. Give it a week and you’ll know all of them.”

Caught this morning, on the table tonight

08 — Find Us

Far enough away to feel it

Getting here is part of the story: an easy hop from Nassau to Congo Town, then a road that keeps getting quieter until it runs out of reasons to be a road. What waits at the end is the west side of South Andros — flats, creeks and open Atlantic.

We’ll walk you through the travel steps when you write. Pack lighter than you think you need to.
