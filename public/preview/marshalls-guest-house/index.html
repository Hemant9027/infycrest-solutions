About Marshall's

Island hospitality,
the old-school way.

Marshall's Guest House sits on Queen's Highway in George Town, looking out across the turquoise sweep of Elizabeth Harbour. It's a simple idea, looked after properly: eight guestrooms, kept cool and spotless, wrapped in warm Caribbean architecture — and a welcome that remembers your name by the second morning.

Days here run on island time. Coffee on the terrace, a boat out to the cays, dinner within walking distance. When you're ready for bed, the trade winds do the talking.

Elizabeth Harbour

Our front yard is legendary.

Elizabeth Harbour is the deep, protected natural harbour that made George Town — long regarded as one of the great anchorages of the Bahamas, and each spring the racecourse of the National Family Island Regatta. From Marshall's, it's simply the view: cruising yachts swinging at anchor, bonefish flats at the edge of the channel, and Stocking Island lying long and green on the horizon.

Some guests come for the boats. Most come back for the quiet.
