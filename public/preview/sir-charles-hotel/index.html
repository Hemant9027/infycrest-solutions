01About Sir Charles Hotel

An independent spirit,a Bahamian soul.

Sir Charles Hotel is an independent island hotel in Nassau — shaped by the easy grace of classic Bahamian hospitality and finished with a modern, boutique sensibility.

Days here move at the island's pace: mornings in soft light, afternoons in palm shade, evenings that stretch a little longer than planned. This is Nassau as it should feel — personal, warm, and quietly elegant.

Classic island hospitality

Independently owned

04Discover Nassau

One island, a hundredshades of blue.

Nassau wears its history in pastel and limestone — a harbour-side capital of Junkanoo drums, hand-carved staircases, straw craft and water so clear it hardly looks real. Our team will happily point you toward their own favourite corners of the island.

Bay Street & the Straw Market

Handwoven straw craft, pastel storefronts, and the easy bustle of a working island capital.

Pastel Streets of Old Nassau

Colonial facades in mint, blush and butter yellow — and the hand-carved Queen's Staircase nearby.

Arawak Cay & Island Flavour

Conch shacks and cookshops serve the islands' signature flavour the way locals love it.

Out on the Blue

Charter a day sail and watch the sea shift through a hundred shades of turquoise and indigo.
